If you are planning to install rooftop solar in 2026, the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the most important financial scheme to understand. Launched with a ₹75,021 crore budget, it aims to provide up to 300 units of free electricity per month to 1 crore Indian households.

But how much money do you actually get back? The Central Financial Assistance (CFA) is strictly based on the capacity of the system you install.

## The 2026 Subsidy Structure
The subsidy is capped at a maximum of ₹78,000, transferred directly to your bank account (DBT) after the installation is complete and inspected by your local DISCOM.

* **Up to 2 kW:** You receive ₹30,000 per kW. (Total: ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system).
* **For the 3rd kW:** You receive an additional ₹18,000. (Total: ₹78,000 for a 3 kW system).
* **Above 3 kW:** The central subsidy caps at ₹78,000. Whether you install a 4 kW or a 10 kW system, the central government payout will not exceed this amount.

## State Top-Ups
Depending on where you live, you might be eligible for additional funds. For example, Uttar Pradesh offers an extra ₹15,000 per kW (up to ₹30,000).
