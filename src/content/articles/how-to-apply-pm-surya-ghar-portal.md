Getting your rooftop solar subsidy isn't automatic—you must follow a strict digital process through the national portal. A single misstep can delay your payout by months.

Here is the exact step-by-step process for homeowners in 2026:

1. **Portal Registration:** Visit the official pmsuryaghar.gov.in portal. You will need your electricity consumer number (found on your latest bill), your mobile number, and your email address.

2. **Feasibility Approval:** Submit your application detailing your roof space and desired kW capacity. Your local DISCOM will review this. Do not buy any equipment until this is approved.

3. **Select an Empanelled Vendor:** You must use an MNRE-approved vendor. If you use an unregistered local electrician, your subsidy will be automatically rejected.

4. **Installation and Net Metering:** Once installed, your vendor will submit the technical details. You then apply for a net meter through the portal, which the DISCOM will physically install to track your grid exports.

5. **Subsidy Disbursement:** After the net meter is active, a commissioning certificate is generated. Upload your cancelled cheque to the portal, and the subsidy will hit your bank account within 30 working days.
